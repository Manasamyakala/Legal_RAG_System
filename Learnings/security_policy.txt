Acme Corp Internal Handbook -- Information Security Policy

Section 9.1: Password Requirements

All employee accounts must use passwords at least 12 characters long, containing a mix of uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols. Passwords must be rotated every 90 days.

Section 9.4: Two-Factor Authentication

Two-factor authentication (2FA) is mandatory for all accounts with access to production systems or customer data. Acceptable 2FA methods include an authenticator app or a hardware security key; SMS-based 2FA is discouraged due to SIM-swapping risk.

Section 9.7: Incident Reporting

Any suspected security incident, including a lost device or a suspicious email, must be reported to security@acmecorp.example within 24 hours of discovery.
