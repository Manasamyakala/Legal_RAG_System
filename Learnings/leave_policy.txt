Acme Corp Internal Handbook -- Leave Policy

Section 7.1: Paid Time Off

Full-time employees accrue 1.5 days of paid time off (PTO) per month, up to a maximum of 18 days per calendar year. Unused PTO up to 5 days may be carried over into the next calendar year; any balance beyond that is forfeited on December 31st.

Section 7.3: Sick Leave

Employees receive 10 paid sick days per year, separate from PTO. Sick leave does not carry over between years.

Section 7.5: Parental Leave

Employees who have completed at least 6 months of continuous employment are eligible for 12 weeks of paid parental leave following the birth or adoption of a child.
